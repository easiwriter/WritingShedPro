Subject: SwiftDataEnsembleContainer fails to attach on Mac Catalyst with EnsembleError.hardwareIdentityUnknown while iOS devices sync successfully

Hello,

We are integrating Ensembles3 into a SwiftData app and are seeing a Mac Catalyst-only attach/sync failure. iOS devices sync successfully using the same CloudKit container, but the Mac Catalyst build cannot attach to the ensemble and repeatedly reports EnsembleError raw value 220, which appears to map to hardwareIdentityUnknown.

Environment

App: Writing Shed Pro
Bundle ID: com.appworks.writingshedpro
Platform affected: Mac Catalyst
Platforms working: iOS devices
Ensembles package: Ensembles3 3.0.5
Resolved revision: 75ce3f4d2a2038cb5cf76b0ee6a0509ad35f7608
Sync wrapper: EnsemblesSwiftData.SwiftDataEnsembleContainer
Cloud backend: EnsemblesCloudKit.CloudKitFileSystem
CloudKit container: iCloud.com.appworks.writingshedpro
Database: private CloudKit database
SwiftData store URL: app documents directory, writingshed.sqlite
Local event data directory: app documents directory, EnsemblesEventData

Configuration:

let cloudFileSystem = CloudKitFileSystem(
    privateDatabaseForUbiquityContainerIdentifier: "iCloud.com.appworks.writingshedpro",
    schemaVersion: .v2
)

let eventDataDirectory = URL.documentsDirectory.appending(
    path: "EnsemblesEventData",
    directoryHint: .isDirectory
)

let configuration = EnsembleContainerConfiguration(
    autoSyncPolicy: .all,
    timerInterval: 120,
    seedPolicy: .mergeAllData,
    compatibilityMode: .ensembles3,
    localDataRootDirectoryURL: eventDataDirectory
)

let ensemblesContainer = SwiftDataEnsembleContainer(
    name: "WritingShedProConfiguration",
    storeURL: storeURL,
    modelTypes: modelTypes,
    cloudFileSystem: cloudFileSystem,
    configuration: configuration
)

We also call UIApplication.shared.registerForRemoteNotifications() before initializing the container.

What We Tried

1. Verified iCloud account availability on Mac: available.
2. Verified CloudKit container access on Mac: private database accessible, zones visible.
3. Verified iOS devices continue to sync successfully.
4. Forced immediate container.sync() on Mac app launch.
5. Logged didEncounterError, didForceDetach, and didSaveMergeChanges.
6. Cleared only the Mac local SwiftData store and local Ensembles event data after backing them up.
7. Relaunched and allowed the Mac to recreate the local store and reattach/import.

The failure remains: isAttached=false, sync() returns false, and EnsembleError 220 repeats.

Question

Is hardwareIdentityUnknown expected under Mac Catalyst in any known signing/sandbox/entitlement condition?

Specifically:

1. Does Ensembles3 require a keychain access group or other entitlement on Mac Catalyst to persist/read its hardware identity?
2. Does SwiftDataEnsembleContainer expose any supported way to provide a stable custom device/hardware identifier for Catalyst?
3. Is this a known issue with the Catalyst build of Ensembles3 3.0.5?
4. Is there a recommended recovery path when a Catalyst client is stuck at hardwareIdentityUnknown and cannot attach?

At the moment, this appears Mac Catalyst-specific: iOS sync works, CloudKit access works on the Mac, but Ensembles cannot determine the Mac hardware identity and therefore never attaches.
