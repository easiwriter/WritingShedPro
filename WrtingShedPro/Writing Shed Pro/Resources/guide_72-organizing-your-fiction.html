Organizing Your Fiction

Writing Shed Pro uses different organizational structures depending on your fiction class. Each class has its own terminology, but the principles are the same: smaller units of writing are grouped into larger containers for manuscript assembly.

Fiction Class Container Writing Unit
Novel Chapters Scenes
Verse Novel Books Episodes
Short Fiction Stories Scenes

Novels: Chapters and Scenes

Understanding Chapters

Chapters in a Novel project: - Are containers for scenes - Determine the order scenes appear in the manuscript - Can include multiple scenes - Are found in the Chapters area of your project

Creating Chapters

  1. Open Chapters
  2. Tap +
  3. Enter a chapter title (e.g., "Chapter 1: The Beginning")
  4. Tap Create

When to Create Chapters

  • After writing several scenes, or
  • Before writing (if you outline), or
  • During revision as structure emerges

There's no wrong time—do what fits your process.

Adding Scenes to Chapters

From the Scene

  1. Open a scene
  2. Access scene settings
  3. Select Add to Chapter
  4. Choose the chapter

From the Chapter

  1. Open a chapter
  2. Tap Add Scene
  3. Select from available scenes

Drag and Drop

On iPad/Mac: - Drag scenes from the Scenes list - Drop onto a chapter - Scene is added to that chapter

Scene Order Within Chapters

Scenes appear in the chapter in a specific order:

  1. Open the chapter
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag scenes using the handles
  4. Tap Done

The order determines how scenes appear in the assembled manuscript.

Reordering Chapters

  1. Open Chapters
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag chapters using the handles
  4. Tap Done

Renaming Chapters

  1. Open Chapters
  2. Long-press the chapter
  3. Select Rename
  4. Enter the new title
  5. Tap Done

Deleting Chapters

  1. Swipe left on the chapter
  2. Tap Delete
  3. Confirm

Note: Deleting a chapter does not delete the scenes—they remain available in the Scenes list. Only the chapter container is removed.

Chapter Best Practices

Naming Conventions

Some options: - "Chapter 1", "Chapter 2"... (simple) - "Chapter 1: The Storm" (numbered with title) - "The Storm" (title only) - "1" (minimalist)

Choose a style and be consistent.

Chapter Length

There's no rule, but consider: - Reader attention spans - Natural story breaks - Pacing and rhythm

Short chapters create urgency; long chapters allow immersion.

When Scenes Belong to No Chapter

Scenes don't have to be in chapters: - Keep works-in-progress with Draft status - Use chapters only for manuscript organization - It's okay to have "loose" scenes

Chapters and Manuscript

When you assemble or export your manuscript: - Chapters appear in order - Scenes within chapters appear in order - Chapter titles become headings

Verse Novels: Books and Episodes

Understanding Books

Books in a Verse Novel project work like chapters but are designed for longer verse narratives. Each book contains episodes—individual poems or verse passages that tell part of the story.

Books in Writing Shed Pro: - Are containers for episodes - Determine the order episodes appear in the manuscript - Can include multiple episodes - Are found in the Books area of your project

Creating Books

  1. Open Books
  2. Tap +
  3. Enter a book title (e.g., "Book I: Departure")
  4. Tap Create

Adding Episodes to Books

From the Episode

  1. Open an episode
  2. Access episode settings
  3. Select Add to Book
  4. Choose the book

From the Book

  1. Open a book
  2. Tap Add Episode
  3. Select from available episodes

Drag and Drop

On iPad/Mac: - Drag episodes from the Episodes list - Drop onto a book

Episode Order Within Books

  1. Open the book
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag episodes using the handles
  4. Tap Done

Reordering Books

  1. Open Books
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag books using the handles
  4. Tap Done

Verse Novel Considerations

  • Episodes use the poetry editor with meter and rhyme support
  • Each episode can use a different poetry form
  • Book titles become headings in the assembled manuscript
  • Poetry formatting (line breaks, stanza spacing) is preserved in export

Short Fiction: Stories and Scenes

Understanding Stories

Short Fiction projects have a simpler structure. Scenes are the primary writing unit, and you can optionally group them into stories.

Stories in Writing Shed Pro: - Are containers for scenes - Group related scenes into a single short story - Are found in the Stories area of your project

Creating Stories

  1. Open Stories
  2. Tap +
  3. Enter a story title
  4. Tap Create

Adding Scenes to Stories

From the Scene

  1. Open a scene
  2. Access scene settings
  3. Select Add to Story
  4. Choose the story

From the Story

  1. Open a story
  2. Tap Add Scene
  3. Select from available scenes

Scene Order Within Stories

  1. Open the story
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag scenes using the handles
  4. Tap Done

Short Fiction Manuscript

When assembling a short fiction manuscript: - All scenes with Ready status are included, in order - Scene breaks are indicated between scenes - Stories provide an optional grouping layer

Common Operations

These operations work the same across all fiction classes.

Renaming Containers

  1. Open the container list (Chapters, Books, or Stories)
  2. Long-press the item
  3. Select Rename
  4. Enter the new title
  5. Tap Done

Deleting Containers

  1. Swipe left on the item
  2. Tap Delete
  3. Confirm

Deleting a container never deletes the writing units inside it—scenes and episodes remain available. Only the container is removed.

Viewing Contents

When you open a container: - See all writing units in order - View word counts - Access each unit for editing

Troubleshooting

Scene/Episode Missing from Container

  • Check if it was removed
  • Check all workflow statuses (Draft, Ready, Set Aside)
  • Writing units must be added to containers explicitly

Wrong Order

  • Open the container in Edit mode
  • Drag to correct order

Container Won't Delete

  • Ensure you have access
  • Check for app issues (restart if needed)

A Scene Can Only Belong to One Container

A scene or episode can only belong to one chapter, book, or story. If content spans containers: - Split into multiple scenes/episodes - Or place in the container where it primarily belongs
