Contact Support

Need help with Writing Shed Pro? Here's how to reach us.

Before You Contact Us

Check the Documentation

Many questions are answered in this guide: - FAQ - Common questions - Troubleshooting - Solutions to known issues

Gather Information

Having this information ready helps us help you faster:

  1. Device: iPhone, iPad, or Mac model
  2. OS Version: iOS/iPadOS/macOS version number
  3. App Version: Found in Settings → About
  4. Description: What happened? What did you expect?
  5. Steps: How can we reproduce the issue?
  6. Screenshots: If helpful, include images

How to Contact Us

In-App Support

  1. Open Writing Shed Pro
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Support or Contact Us
  4. Choose whether you're reporting a bug, making a suggestion, or asking a question
  5. Fill out the form with your details
  6. Tap Send

Your request will be answered immediately. Your device and app information is included automatically to help diagnose any issues.

If the response doesn't fully resolve your issue, you can optionally tap Ask Developer to send an email directly to the developer for further assistance.

Feature Requests

Have an idea to improve Writing Shed Pro?

Submitting Ideas

Use the Contact Support form in Settings and choose Suggestion as the report type.

Tell us: - What feature you'd like - How you'd use it - Why it would help your writing

What Happens to Requests

  • We read every request
  • Popular requests influence development priorities
  • We can't implement everything, but we appreciate all feedback

Bug Reports

Found something broken?

Good Bug Reports Include

  1. What happened: Exact behavior you saw
  2. What you expected: What should have happened
  3. Steps to reproduce: How to make it happen again
  4. Frequency: Every time? Sometimes? Once?
  5. Workaround: Did you find a way around it?

Example Bug Report

Subject: Footnote numbering wrong after delete

What happened: After deleting the second footnote, the third footnote is still labeled "3" instead of "2".

Steps: 1. Create a document with 3 footnotes 2. Delete the second footnote 3. Observe remaining footnotes

Expected: Remaining footnotes should be numbered 1 and 2.

Device: iPad Pro 11", iPadOS 17.4 App version: 1.2.3

Privacy Note

When you contact support: - We use your information only to help you - We may ask for additional details - We don't share your information with third parties - Diagnostic logs never contain your document content

Emergency Data Issues

Lost Important Work?

  1. Don't panic
  2. Check the Trash in-app
  3. Check other devices
  4. Check iCloud Drive in Files app
  5. Contact us immediately—we may be able to help

Time-Sensitive Issues

If you have a deadline, use the Contact Support form and mention the time constraint. If the immediate response isn't sufficient, tap Ask Developer and mention "URGENT" so we can prioritize accordingly.

Thank You

Your feedback makes Writing Shed Pro better. Whether it's a bug report, feature request, or question, we appreciate you taking the time to reach out.
