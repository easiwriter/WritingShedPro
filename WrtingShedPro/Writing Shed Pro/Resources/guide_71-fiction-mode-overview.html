Fiction Mode Overview

Fiction projects in Writing Shed Pro provide specialized tools for writing novels and short stories. This overview explains the features available and how to use them effectively.

What Makes Fiction Mode Special

Fiction mode understands the unique needs of fiction writers:

  • Scene-based writing: Build your story scene by scene
  • Chapter organization: Group scenes into chapters (novels)
  • Plot tracking: Map your story structure
  • Character and location management: Keep details consistent
  • Manuscript assembly: Combine everything for export

Creating a Fiction Project

  • From the project list, tap +
  • Enter a project name
  • Select Fiction as the project type
  • Choose a fiction class:

    • - Novel: Long-form fiction with chapters

    - Short Fiction: Stories without chapter structure

  • Optionally select a story structure
  • Tap Create

    • Fiction Classes

    Novel

    For longer works with chapter structure:

    • Chapters contain scenes
    • Chapters folder organizes your book
    • Full manuscript assembly

    Short Fiction

    For stories without chapters:

    • Scenes are the primary unit
    • Simpler organization
    • Direct manuscript export

    You cannot change the class after project creation.

    Story Structures

    Choose a structure to help organize your plot:

    Freeform

    No predefined stages—organize your plot however you like.

    Three-Act Structure

    Classic narrative structure:

  • Act 1 - Setup: Introduce world and characters, inciting incident
  • Act 2 - Confrontation: Rising action, complications
  • Act 3 - Resolution: Climax and conclusion

  • Refusal of the Call

  • Tests, Allies, Enemies
  • Approach to the Inmost Cave

  • Return with the Elixir

    Core Concepts

    Scenes

    The fundamental unit of fiction. Each scene:

    • Is a single continuous action
    • Has a clear outcome
    • Advances the story
    • Lives in Draft, Ready, or Set Aside

    Chapters (Novel only)

    Containers for scenes:

    • Group related scenes
    • Determine reading order
    • Organize your book structure

    Plot Elements

    Story beats and events:

    • Major and minor plot points
    • Linked to story structure stages
    • Connected to scenes

    Characters

    Your story's people:

    • Names and descriptions
    • Relationships
    • Arcs and development

    Locations

    Where your story happens:

    • Settings and places
    • Descriptions
    • Atmosphere notes

    Fiction Workflow

    1. Plan (Optional)

    • Create plot elements for your story structure
    • Define main characters and locations
    • Outline chapters

    2. Draft

    • Write scenes in the Draft folder
    • Link scenes to plot elements
    • Connect to characters and locations

    3. Organize

    • Move finished scenes to Ready
    • Arrange scenes into chapters
    • Refine your structure

    4. Export

    • Assemble the manuscript
    • Export to PDF or RTF
    • Share or submit
