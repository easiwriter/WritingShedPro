Chapter Management

In Novel projects, chapters organize your scenes into a reading order. This guide covers creating, organizing, and working with chapters.

Understanding Chapters

Chapters in Writing Shed Pro: - Are containers for scenes - Determine the order scenes appear in the manuscript - Can include multiple scenes - Live in the Chapters folder

Creating Chapters

From the Chapters Folder

  1. Open the Chapters folder
  2. Tap +
  3. Enter a chapter title (e.g., "Chapter 1: The Beginning")
  4. Tap Create

When to Create Chapters

  • After writing several scenes, or
  • Before writing (if you outline), or
  • During revision as structure emerges

There's no wrong time—do what fits your process.

Adding Scenes to Chapters

From the Scene

  1. Open a scene in Draft or Ready
  2. Access scene settings
  3. Select Add to Chapter
  4. Choose the chapter

From the Chapter

  1. Open a chapter
  2. Tap Add Scene
  3. Select from available scenes

Drag and Drop

On iPad/Mac: - Drag scenes from Draft/Ready - Drop onto a chapter - Scene is added to that chapter

Scene Order Within Chapters

Scenes appear in the chapter in a specific order:

Reordering Scenes

  1. Open the chapter
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag scenes using the handles
  4. Tap Done

The order determines how scenes appear in the assembled manuscript.

Renaming Chapters

  1. Open the Chapters folder
  2. Long-press the chapter
  3. Select Rename
  4. Enter the new title
  5. Tap Done

Deleting Chapters

  1. Swipe left on the chapter
  2. Tap Delete
  3. Confirm

Note: Deleting a chapter does not delete the scenes—they remain in Draft/Ready. Only the chapter container is removed.

Chapter Order

Chapters appear in your manuscript in a specific order:

Reordering Chapters

  1. Open the Chapters folder
  2. Tap Edit
  3. Drag chapters using the handles
  4. Tap Done

Chapter Best Practices

Naming Conventions

Some options: - "Chapter 1", "Chapter 2"... (simple) - "Chapter 1: The Storm" (numbered with title) - "The Storm" (title only) - "1" (minimalist)

Choose a style and be consistent.

Chapter Length

There's no rule, but consider: - Reader attention spans - Natural story breaks - Pacing and rhythm

Short chapters create urgency; long chapters allow immersion.

When Scenes Belong to No Chapter

Scenes don't have to be in chapters: - Keep works-in-progress in Draft - Use chapters only for manuscript organization - It's okay to have "loose" scenes

Multi-Chapter Scenes

A scene can only belong to one chapter. If content spans chapters: - Split into multiple scenes - Or place in the chapter where it primarily belongs

Viewing Chapter Contents

When you open a chapter: - See all scenes in order - View word counts - Access each scene for editing

Chapters and Manuscript

When you assemble or export your manuscript: - Chapters appear in order - Scenes within chapters appear in order - Chapter titles become headings

See Manuscript Formatting for export details.

Troubleshooting

Scene Missing from Chapter

  • Check if it was removed
  • Look in Draft/Ready folders
  • Scenes must be added explicitly

Wrong Scene Order

  • Open chapter in Edit mode
  • Drag to correct order

Chapter Won't Delete

  • Ensure you have access
  • Check for app issues (restart if needed)
