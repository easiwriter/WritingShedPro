Installation

Writing Shed Pro is available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac through the App Store. This guide covers installation and initial setup.

System Requirements

iPhone and iPad

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • iPadOS 17.0 or later
  • Works on all supported iPhone and iPad models

Mac

  • macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later
  • Mac Catalyst app (same app as iOS)
  • Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs

Installing from the App Store

  1. Open the App Store on your device
  2. Search for "Writing Shed Pro"
  3. Tap Get (or the price button)
  4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
  5. Wait for the download to complete

The app icon appears on your Home Screen (iOS/iPadOS) or in Applications (Mac).

First Launch

When you first open Writing Shed Pro:

  1. iCloud Permission: The app requests permission to use iCloud. We recommend enabling this for:
  2. Automatic backup of all your work
  3. Seamless sync between your devices

  4. No data loss if you lose or replace a device

  5. Notifications: Optional push notifications for submission reminders and deadlines

  6. Empty Project List: You'll see an empty project list, ready for you to create your first project

iCloud Setup

Writing Shed Pro uses iCloud to store and sync your projects. To ensure this works:

  1. Sign in to iCloud: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud
  2. Enable iCloud Drive: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → iCloud Drive: On
  3. Enable Writing Shed Pro: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud → Writing Shed Pro: On

Without iCloud

You can use Writing Shed Pro without iCloud: - Projects are stored locally on your device - No sync between devices - Risk of data loss if device is lost or damaged - We strongly recommend enabling iCloud

Updating the App

Writing Shed Pro updates automatically if you have automatic updates enabled. To update manually:

  1. Open the App Store
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right
  3. Scroll down to see available updates
  4. Tap Update next to Writing Shed Pro

Reinstalling

If you delete and reinstall Writing Shed Pro: - With iCloud enabled: All your projects will sync back automatically - Without iCloud: Your local projects will be lost

Multiple Devices

Writing Shed Pro works across all your Apple devices:

  1. Install on each device using the same Apple ID
  2. Ensure iCloud is enabled on all devices
  3. Your projects appear automatically on each device
  4. Changes sync within seconds

Sync Conflict Resolution

If you edit the same file on two devices simultaneously: - The most recent changes are preserved - Earlier changes are saved as a version - No data is ever lost

Troubleshooting Installation

App Won't Download

  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure you have enough storage space
  • Try restarting the App Store (swipe up to close, reopen)
  • Restart your device

App Won't Open

  • Try force-quitting and reopening
  • Restart your device
  • Delete and reinstall the app (if iCloud is enabled, no data is lost)

iCloud Sync Not Working

See iCloud Sync for detailed troubleshooting.
