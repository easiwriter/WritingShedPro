# Privacy Policy

**Writing Shed Pro**
Effective Date: 20 February 2026

---

## Introduction

Writing Shed Pro ("the App") is developed and published by AppWorks. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy: **Writing Shed Pro does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

## Information We Do Not Collect

Writing Shed Pro does **not** collect, transmit, or have access to:

- Your name, email address, or contact information
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data
- Photos, camera, microphone, or health data
- Browsing history or search queries

No account or sign-in is required to use the App.

## Your Writing Data

All content you create in Writing Shed Pro — projects, files, folders, stylesheets, comments, footnotes, references, submissions, and all associated metadata — is stored **on your device** using Apple's SwiftData framework.

If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs automatically between your devices using **Apple's CloudKit** service. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's infrastructure and is subject to [Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We have no server-side access to your iCloud data. We cannot read, access, or retrieve your writing.

If you disable iCloud, your data remains solely on your device.

## In-App Purchases

Writing Shed Pro offers optional in-app purchases (individual genre modules, an all-in bundle, and subscriptions) processed entirely through **Apple's App Store**. All payment and transaction data is handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID.

Purchase details are subject to [Apple's App Store Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Third-Party Services

Writing Shed Pro does **not** integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking frameworks. The App does not contain any third-party SDKs that collect user data.

The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary used for syllable counting and rhyme detection is bundled locally within the App and requires no network connection.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any third party. There is no data to share.

## Children's Privacy

Writing Shed Pro does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the App collects no personal data, it is suitable for users of all ages.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your writing data is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account (if enabled). You can delete the App and all its local data at any time. To remove iCloud data, manage your iCloud storage through your device's Settings.

## Export and Portability

Writing Shed Pro allows you to export your work in standard formats including PDF, Word (.docx), RTF, HTML, Markdown, Fountain, and Final Draft (.fdx). You retain full ownership and control of everything you create.

## Security

Your on-device data is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). iCloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. We recommend keeping your device software up to date and using a strong passcode.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update it within the App and on our support page. The "Effective Date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
