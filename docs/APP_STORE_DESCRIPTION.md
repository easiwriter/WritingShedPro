## Description

Writing Shed Pro is a powerful, all-in-one writing app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac — built for poets, novelists, playwrights, and prose writers who want professional tools without the complexity.

**ONE APP. EVERY GENRE.**
Create projects tailored to your craft — Poetry, Fiction (novel or short fiction), Drama, Prose, or Manual. Each project type comes with smart templates, specialized tools, and an organized folder structure so you can focus on the words, not the filing.

**WRITE WITH CONFIDENCE**
A rich text editor with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, and a full stylesheet system lets you define and apply consistent styles across your entire project. Real-time word and line counts, inline comments, footnotes, hyperlinks, and image support give you everything you need — right where you need it.

**POETRY TOOLS THAT UNDERSTAND METER**
Choose from dozens of established forms — Sonnet, Haiku, Villanelle, Tanka, and more — or create your own. A real-time syllable counter powered by the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, stress pattern analysis, and a poetry metrics dashboard help you nail the rhythm every time.

**FICTION TOOLS THAT SHAPE YOUR STORY**
Organize your novel into chapters and scenes. Build character and location profiles with unified details notes, and map plot outlines. Choose from Freeform, Three-Act, or Monomyth story structures. Move scenes between chapters as your story evolves.

**DRAMA TOOLS FOR STAGE AND SCREEN**
Write screenplays and stage plays using Drama Markup Language — a lightweight, readable syntax that automatically formats character names, dialogue, and stage directions. The same source renders correctly as either film/screenplay or stage play format.

**SEE IT ON THE PAGE**
A true WYSIWYG paginated preview shows exactly how your document will look when printed. Configure paper size, margins, headers, footers, and page numbers for each project independently.

**ASSEMBLE YOUR MANUSCRIPT**
Compile your entire project — front matter, body, and back matter — into a single formatted manuscript. Auto-generated table of contents, index, glossary, citations, bibliography, and table of figures are built in.

**TRACK SUBMISSIONS & PUBLICATIONS**
Keep a record of where you've sent your work, when, and what happened. Manage publication venues, group files into submission packets, and never lose track of a response again.

**IMPORT & EXPORT YOUR WORK**
- Export your assembled manuscript as a professionally formatted PDF
- Export individual files as Word (.docx), RTF, HTML, or Markdown
- Import Word (.docx), RTF, and Markdown files into any project
- Import and export Fountain (.fountain) and Final Draft (.fdx) files for drama projects
- Share complete projects in the native .wsp format
- Migrate from the original Writing Shed app with smart database import

**PRINT LIKE A PRO**
Print single files, entire collections, or submission packets with professional formatting, footnotes, and page layout — on iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

**SYNCED ACROSS ALL YOUR DEVICES**
Your projects, files, formatting, and metadata sync seamlessly via iCloud. Start a poem on your iPhone, revise it on your iPad, and print it from your Mac.

**LEARN AS YOU GO**
A comprehensive built-in user guide with tutorials walks you through everything from getting started to assembling a professional manuscript. Smart contextual tips appear at just the right moment to help you discover features — from your first project through advanced workflows. All tips are dismissible and can be reset at any time.

**TRY IT FREE**
Explore every project type at no cost — with limited use (one project and one file per type). When you're ready, unlock full capabilities by genre — Prose Writer, Poetry Writer, Fiction Writer, Drama Writer — or get everything with the All-in Bundle.
