# Introduction

Welcome to Writing Shed Pro, the professional writing app designed for authors, poets, playwrights, and fiction writers. Whether you're crafting your first haiku or your tenth novel, Writing Shed Pro provides the tools you need to write, oWSP Is Writing Shed Pro?

Writing Shed Pro is a complete writing environment that runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It's built from the ground up for writers who take their craft seriously. Unlike generic word processors, every feature is designed with creative writing in mind.

## Key Features at a Glance

### Project-Based Organization
Keep all your writing organized in projects. Each project contains folders and files that sync seamlessly across all your devices via iCloud.

### Specialized Project Types
- **Prose**: General-purpose writing for essays, articles, and more
- **Poetry**: Smart poetry tools with syllable counting, rhyme detection, and form templates
- **Fiction**: Novel, short fiction, and verse novel project types with scene and chapter management, plot outlining, and character tracking
- **Drama**: Script formatting with automatic dialogue layout

### Professional Writing Tools
- Rich text formatting with customizable stylesheets
- Footnotes with automatic numbering
- Comments for notes and revision tracking
- Images with captions and alignment options
- Powerful search and replace across your entire project

### Publishing Ready
- Export to PDF, RTF, and other formats
- Professional pagination with headers and footers
- Submission tracking for magazines and competitions
- Print directly from the app

## Who Is Writing Shed Pro For?

**Poets** who need syllable counting, stress pattern analysis, and templates for traditional forms like sonnets, haiku, and villanelles.

**Fiction Writers** who want to organize scenes, track plot elements, and manage characters and locations.

**Playwrights and Screenwriters** who need professional script formatting with support for both stage plays and screenplays.

**Essayists and Bloggers** who want a distraction-free writing environment with powerful formatting tools.

**Anyone** who takes their writing seriously and wants a professional tool that grows with them.
