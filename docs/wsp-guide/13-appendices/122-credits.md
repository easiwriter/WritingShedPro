## Design Philosophy

Writing Shed Pro was built on these principles:

### Writers First
Every feature is designed with working writers in mind. Tools should support creativity, not interrupt it.

### Cross-Platform Consistency
Whether you're on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, Writing Shed Pro feels familiar and works the same way.

### Your Words, Your Way
From prose to poetry to drama, the app adapts to your writing style and project needs.

### Privacy Respected
Your writing is yours. We don't read it, analyze it, or share it.
