# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Writing Shed Pro.

## General

### What is Writing Shed Pro?
Writing Shed Pro is a professional writing app for creative writers. It supports prose, poetry, fiction, and drama with specialized tools for each.

### What devices does it run on?
- iPhone running iOS 17 or later
- iPad running iPadOS 17 or later
- Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later

### Do my files sync between devices?
Yes. With iCloud enabled, your projects sync automatically across all your Apple devices.

### Is my data backed up?
iCloud provides automatic backup. For extra safety, you can export important work as PDF or RTF.

### Can I use it offline?
Yes. You can write offline. Changes sync when you reconnect.

## Projects

### What's the difference between project types?
- **Prose**: General writing with no specialized tools
- **Poetry**: Syllable counting, rhyme detection, verse tools
- **Fiction**: Chapters, scenes, plot tracking
- **Drama**: Script formatting with DML

### Can I change a project's type?
No. Project type is set at creation. You can copy content to a new project of a different type.

### What are Draft, Ready, and Trash?
- **Draft**: Work in progress
- **Ready**: Finished work
- **Trash**: Deleted files (can be recovered)

### How do I share a project?
Export your project or individual files as PDF or RTF, then share using the standard share sheet.

## Writing

### Does it have spell check?
Yes. The system spell checker is active while you write.

### Is there a word count goal?
Word count is displayed but there's no built-in goal tracking.

### How do I add images?
In the editor, use Insert → Image. You can add from Photos, Files, or Camera.

### Are footnotes supported?
Yes. Insert footnotes through the Format menu or toolbar. They appear at page bottom.

## Poetry

### How accurate is the syllable counter?
Very accurate for standard English. Some proper nouns and unusual words may need manual counting.

### What's the CMU dictionary?
The Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary provides pronunciation data for syllable counting and rhyme detection.

### Can I create my own poetry forms?
You can select from built-in forms. Custom form creation may be added in future updates.

## Fiction

### How do chapters and scenes work?
Scenes are individual files. Chapters are folders that contain scenes. You control the structure.

### What are plot elements?
Plot elements (Setups, Conflicts, Resolutions) help you track story structure. Scenes can link to plot elements.

### Is there character management?
Basic character notes are supported. Full character management may be added in future updates.

## Drama

### What is DML?
Drama Markup Language—a simple way to write scripts using plain text that formats automatically.

### Can I export to Final Draft?
Currently, exports are PDF and RTF. Industry-specific formats may be added later.

### Film or stage—which should I choose?
Choose based on your target production. You can preview both formats from the same DML source.

## Export and Printing

### What formats can I export?
- PDF (recommended for most uses)
- RTF (Rich Text Format)

### Can I export to Word (.docx)?
RTF exports can be opened in Word. Direct .docx export may be added later.

### How do I print?
Use the standard Print option. Works on Mac and iPad with compatible printers.

## Sync and Storage

### How much iCloud storage do I need?
Depends on your usage. Text uses minimal storage. Images use more. Most users need less than 1GB.

### Can I use it without iCloud?
Yes, but sync between devices won't work. Your data stays on the device.

### I have a new device—where's my data?
Sign into iCloud with the same Apple ID. Install Writing Shed Pro. Your projects will appear.

### Why is sync slow?
First sync may take time. Large projects take longer. Poor internet slows sync. Wait and check again.

## Technical

### Can I access files in the Files app?
Writing Shed Pro uses its own storage. Export files if you need them in Files.

### Does it work with external keyboards?
Yes. Full keyboard shortcut support on iPad and Mac.

### Is there a web version?
No. Writing Shed Pro is a native Apple app only.

### Can I use it on Windows or Android?
No. It's exclusive to Apple platforms.
