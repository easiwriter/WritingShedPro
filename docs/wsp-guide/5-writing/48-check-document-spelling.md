# Check Document Spelling

Writing Shed Pro includes a document-wide spelling checker for reviewing a complete file. It uses Apple's spelling dictionaries installed on your device.

## Opening the Spelling Checker

- **iPad and Mac**: Tap the **Check Document Spelling** button (the text-and-checkmark icon) in the editor toolbar
- **iPhone**: Open the editor's actions menu and choose **Check Document Spelling**
- **Hardware keyboard**: Press **⌘;**

The spelling checker is available in Edit Mode, not Page Preview. Opening it closes Find and Replace if that bar is visible.

## Reviewing Spelling

The checker scans the document and highlights words it does not recognise. The current word is selected and scrolled into view, and the bar shows its position in the results, such as **2 of 7**.

- Use the up and down arrows to move between detected words
- Open **Suggestions** to choose a replacement
- Tap **Replace** to change the current word while retaining its formatting
- Tap **Ignore** to skip this occurrence during the current pass
- Tap **Ignore All** to skip every occurrence of that word for the current checker session
- Tap the globe button to choose another installed spelling language; the document is rescanned automatically

Replacements can be reversed with Undo. The checker also rescans while you edit, keeping its results aligned with the current text. If no possible replacement is available, the Suggestions menu reports **No suggestions**.

When the checker reports **No spelling errors found**, use **Check Again** to run another scan, or close the bar with the **×** button.

> **Note**: Names, invented terms, specialist vocabulary, and intentional dialect may be reported because they are not in the selected Apple dictionary. Use **Ignore All** when you want to leave such a word unchanged during this checking session.
