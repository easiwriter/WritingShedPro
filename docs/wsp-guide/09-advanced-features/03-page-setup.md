# Page Setup

Page Setup controls how your documents appear when exported or printed. Configure paper size, margins, headers, footers, and more.

## Accessing Page Setup

1. Open your project
2. Go to **Project Settings**
3. Select **Page Setup**

Page setup applies to the entire project.

## Paper Size

Choose from standard paper sizes:

| Size | Dimensions | Common Use |
|------|------------|------------|
| **Letter** | 8.5 × 11 inches | US standard |
| **A4** | 210 × 297 mm | International standard |
| **Legal** | 8.5 × 14 inches | US legal documents |
| **A5** | 148 × 210 mm | Half of A4 |
| **Custom** | Your dimensions | Special needs |

### Choosing a Size
- Match your printer's paper
- Match submission requirements
- Consider your audience's location (US uses Letter, most of world uses A4)

## Orientation

- **Portrait**: Taller than wide (default)
- **Landscape**: Wider than tall

Most writing uses portrait. Landscape might suit wide tables or special layouts.

## Margins

Set the space between page edge and content:

### Margin Settings
- **Top**: Space at page top
- **Bottom**: Space at page bottom
- **Left** (Inside): Left margin
- **Right** (Outside): Right margin

### Common Margin Sizes
| Use Case | Margins |
|----------|---------|
| Standard manuscript | 1 inch all sides |
| Printing with binding | 1.25" left, 1" others |
| More content per page | 0.75" all sides |

### Considerations
- Larger margins = more whitespace, easier reading
- Smaller margins = more content per page
- Some printers can't print to the edge

## Headers and Footers

Add information at the top or bottom of every page:

### Header Content
- Author name
- Document title
- Custom text

### Footer Content
- Page numbers
- Date
- Custom text

### Page Number Formats
- Plain numbers (1, 2, 3)
- With total (1 of 10)
- Roman numerals (i, ii, iii)

### Positioning
- Left, Center, or Right aligned
- First page can differ from others

## First Page Different

For many documents:
- First page has no header/footer (or different one)
- Title page stands alone
- Numbering starts on page 2

Toggle **First Page Different** to enable this.

## Pagination View

Preview your page setup:

1. Open a file
2. Switch to **Pagination View**
3. See pages as they'll appear
4. Verify margins, headers, footers
5. Check page breaks

## Page Setup for Different Uses

### Standard Manuscript
- Letter or A4 paper
- 1-inch margins all around
- Header: Name - Title - Page number
- Double-spaced body text
- First page: Title, author, word count

### Poetry Collection
- Comfortable margins (1-1.25 inches)
- Page numbers in footer
- Title at top of each poem page

### Script
- Industry-specific margins
- Courier 12pt font
- Specific header/footer format

### Personal Reading
- Whatever you prefer
- Consider your reading environment

## Page Breaks

Control where pages break:

### Automatic Breaks
Pages break naturally when content fills a page.

### Manual Breaks
Insert page breaks to force new pages:
- Before chapters
- After title pages
- Where you need control

### Widow/Orphan Control
Avoid:
- Single lines at page bottom (orphan)
- Single lines at page top (widow)
Pagination engine handles this automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### Content Cut Off
- Margins may be too small for printer
- Check "printable area" of your printer
- Increase margins slightly

### Wrong Paper Size
- Change in Page Setup
- Ensure it matches printer settings

### Headers/Footers Missing
- Check they're enabled
- Check "First Page Different" setting
- Preview in Pagination View

### Page Numbers Wrong
- Check starting number setting
- Check first page settings
- Verify in Pagination View
