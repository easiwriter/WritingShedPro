# Manuscript Formatting

When your novel or story is ready, Writing Shed Pro assembles your scenes and chapters into a professional manuscript. This guide covers formatting options and the assembly process.

## Manuscript Assembly

### For Novels
1. Chapters are assembled in order
2. Scenes within each chapter are combined
3. Chapter headings are added
4. The result is a single, continuous manuscript

### For Short Fiction
1. Scenes are assembled in order
2. Scene breaks are indicated
3. The result is a complete story

## Assembling Your Manuscript

### Access Assembly
1. Open your fiction project
2. Find **Manuscript** or **Assemble** in the menu
3. Select assembly options
4. Generate the manuscript

### What Gets Included
- All scenes in chapters (Novel)
- All scenes in Ready folder, in order (Short Fiction)
- Applied formatting from your stylesheet

## Formatting Options

### Chapter Headings
Control how chapters appear:
- Show chapter number ("Chapter 1")
- Show chapter title ("The Storm")
- Both ("Chapter 1: The Storm")
- Custom format

### Scene Breaks
When scenes transition within a chapter:
- Blank line (simple)
- Symbol (e.g., * * * or §)
- No visible break

### First Paragraph Style
First paragraph of scenes or chapters can have special formatting:
- No indent (traditional)
- Drop cap (decorative)
- Standard indent

## Industry Standard Formatting

For submissions, consider standard manuscript format:

### Typical Requirements
- Double-spaced text
- 12pt Courier or Times New Roman
- 1-inch margins
- Header with name, title, page number
- Word count on first page
- Scene breaks indicated

### Applying Standard Format
1. Configure your stylesheet for manuscript style
2. Set page setup for letter/A4
3. Export to PDF or RTF

## Preview Before Export

Always preview your assembled manuscript:
1. Open the manuscript preview
2. Check chapter breaks
3. Verify scene order
4. Review formatting
5. Look for issues

## Export Options

Once assembled, export your manuscript:

### PDF
- Fixed format
- Looks identical everywhere
- Good for reading copies
- Standard for many submissions

### RTF
- Editable by recipients
- Compatible with Word
- Some formatting may vary
- Good for editor collaboration

See [Publishing](../07-publishing/01-export-options.md) for full export details.

## Formatting for Different Purposes

### Agent/Publisher Submission
- Standard manuscript format
- Clean, professional appearance
- Follow specific guidelines if given

### Beta Readers
- Easy-to-read formatting
- May include page numbers
- Consider PDF for consistency

### Personal Review
- Your preference
- Consider larger text for proofing
- Print for marking up

### Self-Publishing
- Will need additional formatting
- This is a starting point
- Platform-specific requirements vary

## Troubleshooting

### Scenes Out of Order
- Check chapter scene order
- Verify chapter order
- Review in manuscript preview

### Missing Scenes
- Ensure scenes are in chapters (Novel)
- Ensure scenes are in Ready (Short Fiction)
- Check that scenes aren't in Draft/Set Aside

### Formatting Issues
- Review your stylesheet
- Check paragraph styles
- Verify page setup settings

### Chapter Breaks Wrong
- Check chapter heading settings
- Ensure chapters are defined
- Review in pagination view

## Tips for Professional Manuscripts

### Consistency
- Same fonts throughout
- Consistent spacing
- Uniform headers

### Readability
- Adequate margins
- Reasonable line spacing
- Clear scene breaks

### Professionalism
- No typos in headers
- Correct page numbers
- Clean title page

### Follow Guidelines
- Check submission requirements
- Adjust format as needed
- When in doubt, ask
