# Manuscript Analyst

Manuscript Analyst is an optional editorial feedback service for Poetry, Prose, Fiction, Drama, Short Fiction, and Verse Novels. It reads your work as an attentive second reader and returns a substantial response to the writing as a whole, followed by a closer examination of the craft.

The analysis is intended to support your own judgement. It does not score the writing, make changes automatically, or present one interpretation as the only correct reading.

## What You Receive

A review contains a **First Reading**, a **Writing Pattern Assessment**, and a **Second Reading**. These are parts of one analysis; you do not need to submit the work twice.

### First Reading

The First Reading gives a generous initial response to the piece. It considers:

- The immediate impression made on an attentive reader
- The apparent aims, mood, voice, and tone
- The most effective or distinctive features
- The emotional or narrative movement
- Questions that may be worth carrying into a closer reading

The First Reading is deliberately readerly rather than clinical. A quiet, conversational, humorous, domestic, or accessible piece is considered on its own terms instead of being pushed towards a more conventionally "literary" style.

### Second Reading

The Second Reading looks more closely at the elements that genuinely shape the piece. Depending on the writing type and the submitted text, it may discuss:

- Voice, tone, diction, and clarity
- Structure, pacing, lineation, or scene movement
- Imagery, rhythm, sound, and literary devices
- Character, relationship, motivation, dialogue, or point of view
- Emotional development and thematic implications
- The effectiveness of the opening and ending
- Details that should be preserved as well as points worth reconsidering

Each section can include a source location, close reading, something worth preserving or testing, an explanation of why it matters, and a revision priority. Not every section is a fault. When a passage is working well, the Analyst can explain why it succeeds and recommend preserving it.

The Second Reading ends with an overall assessment of the piece's distinctive qualities and, where appropriate, its most important revision priority.

### Adapting to the Writing Type

The Analyst changes its emphasis to suit the project:

- **Poetry**: voice, imagery, diction, line breaks, rhythm, sound, form, restraint, and emotional coherence
- **Prose**: clarity, organisation, transitions, exposition, voice, and reader engagement
- **Fiction and Short Fiction**: character, point of view, scene effectiveness, continuity, pacing, stakes, and endings
- **Drama**: dialogue, subtext, character objectives, scene dynamics, escalation, and stage clarity
- **Verse Novels**: poetic technique and narrative movement together

If a poetic form is declared and form preservation is enabled, revision guidance should respect the existing line count, stanza pattern, refrain positions, and structural constraints.

## Before You Begin

You need:

- An active Manuscript Analyst subscription or eligible Analyst trial
- An internet connection
- Text in the file you want to analyse

Manuscript Analyst sends the selected writing to the analysis service when you request a review. It does not analyse your work continuously in the background.

## Using Manuscript Analyst

1. Open the file you want to review.
2. Start analysis from the editor toolbar:
   - **iPad or Mac**: tap **Analyze** (the text magnifying-glass icon) in the top-right toolbar.
   - **iPhone**: tap **...** in the toolbar, then tap **Analyze**.
3. Analysis starts immediately after you tap the button.
4. If you do not have access, the Manuscript Analyst subscription screen appears. Complete the Apple purchase or trial confirmation to continue.
5. Keep the analysis screen open while the review is prepared.
6. Read the First Reading, Writing Pattern Assessment, and Second Reading.
7. Decide which observations or revision questions are useful for your intentions.
8. Return to the editor and make any changes manually.

## Working with the Review

The review screen provides several controls:

- Tap a Second Reading section to expand or collapse it.
- Sort sections by **Reading Order**, **Severity**, or **Category**.
- Choose **Larger Text** or **Standard Text** from the review menu.
- Mark a revision point as addressed when you have considered it.
- Choose **Hide Addressed** to concentrate on the remaining points.
- Choose **Copy Full Review** to copy the complete response for use in notes or another document.

The copied review includes the First Reading, highlighted focus areas, Writing Pattern Assessment and disclaimer, and every visible Second Reading section.

## Interpreting Revision Priorities

Revision priority describes the possible importance of a point; it is not a score for the quality of the writing.

- **High** is reserved for something that clearly interferes with comprehension, coherence, continuity, or the piece's stated intention.
- **Medium** identifies a meaningful craft question that may affect the reader's experience.
- **Low** covers subtle, optional, or taste-dependent considerations and sections whose main purpose is to recognise a strength.

The Analyst may be uncertain or offer a possibility rather than a verdict. Treat its comments as prompts for your own editorial decisions.

## Writing Pattern Assessment

Writing Pattern Assessment appears between the First Reading and Second Reading. It examines structural and stylistic features in the submitted text, such as unusually uniform sentence construction, repeated paragraph patterns, formulaic transitions, consistently abstract language, repeated conclusions, or abrupt changes in style.

The assessment reports one of the following results:

- **No notable patterns**: the analysis did not find sufficiently distinctive patterns to highlight
- **Some notable patterns**: the analysis found a limited number of relevant patterns
- **Multiple notable patterns**: the analysis found several relevant patterns across the text
- **Inconclusive**: the available signals conflict or do not support a responsible distinction
- **Insufficient text**: the sample is too short or fragmentary for a meaningful assessment

The result also includes a **Low** or **Moderate** confidence label, a summary, and line-referenced observations when relevant. Confidence is deliberately limited because text-only pattern analysis is inherently uncertain.

> **Important:** Writing Pattern Assessment cannot determine who wrote a text or prove that AI was used. A result of **No notable patterns** does not establish human authorship, and notable patterns do not establish AI authorship. Treat the assessment as additional editorial information, not as evidence for academic, employment, publication, or disciplinary decisions.

Poetry, short passages, heavily edited text, translations, and highly formal writing can be particularly difficult to assess because many legitimate writing techniques overlap with patterns sometimes found in generated text.

## What Manuscript Analyst Does Not Do

Manuscript Analyst:

- Does not edit or replace the text in your file
- Does not generate a rewritten version of your manuscript
- Does not publish, export, or submit your work
- Does not determine authorship or prove that AI was used
- Does not replace your own judgement or professional editorial advice

## If Analysis Does Not Start

Check the following:

2. Confirm that the Manuscript Analyst subscription is active.
3. Remember that the core app's 10-Day Free Trial does not include Analyst access.
4. If you have just subscribed, allow the Apple purchase confirmation to finish before closing the subscription screen.
5. If the app says an update is required, install the current App Store version before trying again.
